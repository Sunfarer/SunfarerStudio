Our mission

The Sunfarer mission is to create playful experiences that revolve around sensitive real-world issues, which are not conventionally represented in video games. Our work is not only a form of artistic expression but also a call to players to reflect and take action. Through collaboration and community, we strive to bring out deeply personal stories from those who are the most unheard.

The Sunfarer team

Core values

  • Wellbeing First

    Maintaining a healthy body and mind is what allows us to keep creating. The health of our fellow Sunfarers always comes first before any deadline or goal.

  • Befriending Failure

    Sunfarers are not afraid or ashamed of making mistakes. Failure always comes with an opportunity to learn and grow!

  • Every Voice Matters

    All Sunfarers, no matter their position in the team, are encouraged to voice their ideas, thoughts and opinions, which will always be received with respect and curiosity.

  • Respect and Empathy

    Sunfarers embrace and celebrate diversity. We always meet each other as equals and treat every Sunfarer with respect and kindness. 

  • Human-Made

    People are wonderfully creative and skillful beings. No generative AI will ever be used under Sunfarer.
